## About Me

I'm an Information Technology undergraduate at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education (HCMUTE)**, interested in both **fundamental machine learning research** and building **reproducible AI systems**.

My work explores how models learn, transfer, and generalize across tasks and data distributions. I also enjoy developing practical applications in computer vision, speech processing, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM serving.

## Research Interests

- **Medical AI:** medical image segmentation, robustness, and learning from limited data
- **Continual & Transfer Learning:** catastrophic forgetting, task interference, and domain generalization
- **Computer Vision & Multimodal Learning:** visual question answering, video understanding, and vision-language models
- **Speech & Language AI:** Vietnamese speech recognition, dialect identification, and tool-using agents

## Selected Research Projects

| Project | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[Cross-Dataset Fall Detection](https://github.com/Datyth/cross-dataset-fall-detection)** | Skeleton-based video classification using RTMPose and CTR-GCN. Evaluates generalization across four datasets with leave-one-dataset-out testing. |
| **[Multi-Task Visual Question Answering](https://github.com/Datyth/Multitasks-Learning-VQA)** | A controlled GQA pilot studying task transfer and interference in vision-language models, using Qwen3.5 and parameter-efficient fine-tuning. |
| **[SegCraft: Medical Image Segmentation](https://github.com/Datyth/SegCraft-A-Lightweight-Image-Segmentation-Experiment-Framework)** | A modular, reproducible experiment framework for ISIC 2018 skin-lesion segmentation, with configurable training, evaluation, and experiment tracking. |
| **[Drug–Drug Interaction Continual Learning](https://github.com/UeenHuynh/DDI-CIL)** | Collaborative study of class-incremental learning protocols for imbalanced drug–drug interaction data, including task construction and forgetting analysis. |
